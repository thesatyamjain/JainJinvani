# Brag Plan: जैन जिनवाणी (Jain Jinvani) [Vertical 9:16]

## What is this app?
Jain Jinvani is an authentic, serene digital scripture library, encyclopedia, and daily spiritual companion for the global Jain community, unifying 450+ sacred texts, 64 Vidhans, an interactive 108 Jaap Mala, Samayik timer, and ancient lunisolar calendar.

## The angle
Ancient wisdom tailored for the smartphone generation. Jain Jinvani brings 450+ sacred texts, interactive rosary chanting, and equanimity meditation into a pocket-sized, frosted-glass sanctuary with authentic Devanagari typography.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A deep cosmic midnight canvas glows with radiant amber light as a pure temple bell chime rings, revealing the sacred blessing: "जिनेन्द्र भगवान की शाश्वत अमृतवाणी" followed by the bold golden insignia "जैन जिनवाणी".

## Key moments (the middle)
- **450+ Sacred Grantha Treasury**: Staggered vertical stat stack revealing 450+ texts, 64 Vidhans, 36 Granthas, and VNS 2552 Panchang.
- **Interactive Daily Sadhana**: Centered live 108 Jaap Mala with rotating bead ring and counter ticking up to 108, alongside 48-minute Samayik timer & Niyama tracker.
- **Devanagari Scripture Reader**: Beautiful mobile card featuring Bhaktamar Stotra Verse 1 with Sanskrit original, word meaning (अन्वयार्थ), and instant search.

## Outro / punchline
The golden lotus emblem docks smoothly with radiant ambient light: "जिनेन्द्र भगवान की शाश्वत अमृतवाणी" with the glowing URL pill `jainjinvani.pages.dev` and "100% निःशुल्क • PWA सक्षम".

## User flow worth showing
Mobile entry (Navokar Mantra & Sacred Hero) → Daily Sadhana (108 Jaap Mala interactive counter & Samayik timer) → Scripture Reader (Bhaktamar Stotra & Samayasara with pure typography) → Free web & mobile app access.
